## Findings

- Initial Regular Research Paper limit: 10 pages.
- Abstract: 150–200 words.
- Index terms: up to 10 and alphabetized; no official minimum was found.
- Format: US Letter, single-spaced, double-column, nominal 10-point body.
- Review: single-anonymous.
- Conventional PES supplementary material: prohibited.
- Substantive AI assistance: disclosure in Acknowledgments must identify the
  system, affected sections, and level of use.

The revision prompt's requested “supplement” will therefore be implemented as
detailed reproducibility/QC material, not as a conventional submission
supplement. Essential evidence remains in the main paper.
