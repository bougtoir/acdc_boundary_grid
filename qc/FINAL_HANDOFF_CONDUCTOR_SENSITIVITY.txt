Audit finding (Phase 1)
-----------------------
Installed conductor counts (n AC=4, n DC=3) enter ONLY the normalized
material regularizer: material_index = n x length_km x scale. They do not
affect resistance, ampacity, voltage drop, or capacity. Electrical loss uses
fixed physical wire counts (3-phase AC 3I^2R, bipolar DC 2I^2R), which are
circuit assumptions and are intentionally not parameterized. The 4/3 counts
are therefore a bookkeeping convention, not a physical capability claim.

Results (Phase 3–4)
-------------------
- Architecture class invariant in EVERY cell of EVERY scenario:
  0.00% architecture changes, 0.00% boundary-signature changes vs baseline.
- Architecture distribution identical everywhere: all-AC 62.29%,
  hybrid 27.71%, root-DC 10.00%; mean DC bus fraction 17.10%.
- Paired physical-loss contrast moved only in the 4th significant digit:
  mean -9.5828% to -9.5819% across scenarios.
- Conclusion: the conditional planning value of permitting DC does NOT depend
  on the 4/3 conductor-count convention within the tested accounting range.
  The principal claim survives at the most DC-favorable physical bound (4/2).
- Interpretation limits honestly stated: this is an accounting sensitivity;
  it does not claim equal electrical capability of real conductor sets, and
  does not cover grounding/protection/neutral-return requirements (unmodeled).
