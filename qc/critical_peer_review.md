# Pre-submission critical peer review

## Highest priority: required before submission

1. **Complete human metadata and declarations.**
   - Domain: reproducibility and submission integrity.
   - Risk: desk return or invalid submission.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: high / high / high.
   - Action: replace author, affiliation, correspondence, funding, conflict,
     contribution, prior-publication, and approval placeholders; verify the final AI
     disclosure against the live portal.
2. **Freeze the public archival revision.**
   - Domain: reproducibility.
   - Risk: manuscript and repository can diverge after review.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: medium / high / high.
   - Action: archive the reviewed public commit and add its DOI if required.

## High priority: scientifically important and currently addressed

1. **Keep the contribution focused on attribution rather than universal superiority.**
   - Domain: novelty, logic, and claim strength.
   - Risk: major revision for an overgeneralized DC-benefit claim.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: high / high / high.
   - Resolution: nested contrasts, null regions, and conditional transitions are
     explicit; universal, causal, probability, and absolute-economic claims are absent.
2. **Preserve the distinction between design cells and independent replications.**
   - Domain: statistical design.
   - Risk: pseudo-replication and invalid uncertainty interpretation.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: high / high / high.
   - Resolution: grid frequencies are labeled descriptive, repeated structural outcomes
     are not treated as independent samples, and discrepancy classes are not confidence
     intervals or hypothesis tests.
3. **Do not extend nonlinear validation beyond the evidence.**
   - Domain: method validity and claim strength.
   - Risk: unsupported hybrid-control, unbalance, protection, or reliability claims.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: high / high / high.
   - Resolution: validation is labeled balanced all-AC only; the missing hybrid and
     operational phenomena remain explicit limitations.

## Medium priority

1. **Retain separate attribution, transition, discrepancy, and ablation visuals.**
   - Domain: figures and tables.
   - Risk: mechanism claims become difficult to audit if panels are collapsed.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: medium / medium / high.
   - Resolution: five figures and ten tables each support a distinct evidentiary role;
     all are called out before their captions.
2. **Interpret computational timing as implementation evidence only.**
   - Domain: reproducibility.
   - Risk: machine-load variability could be mistaken for an algorithmic guarantee.
   - Fatality / benefit / feasibility: low / medium / high.
   - Resolution: exactness and analytical complexity are separated from live timings,
     and binary variance is documented.

## Optional

1. Add a graphical abstract only if the submission portal and authors prefer one.
2. Extend future work with measured converter maps, unbalanced hybrid control,
   protection, reliability, and location-specific economics; these require new evidence
   and are not prerequisites for the present mechanism paper.

## Verdict

No unresolved technical issue requires new analysis before review. The package is not
submission-ready until the highest-priority human metadata and archival decisions are
completed.
