# Hostile TSG review: round 2

## Major-claim challenge

The manuscript does not support universal DC superiority, a causal utility effect, a
deployment probability, or an absolute economic recommendation. Its contribution is
the matched attribution design and the conditional architecture-transition evidence.

## Model-validity challenge

The dynamic program is exact only for the declared balanced radial separable
approximation. The manuscript reports all-AC pandapower discrepancy, retains an
indeterminate category, and does not imply hybrid converter-control validation.

## External-validity challenge

The three feeders are benchmark archetypes, the minimum-spanning trees are synthetic,
normalized regularizers are not costs, and the converter ablations are dimensionless
planning scenarios rather than vendor curves.

## Reproducibility challenge

Raw objects are preserved locally with checksums and provenance; public reconstruction
uses scripted acquisition. Scientific values are registry-backed. Live timing and PDF
binary metadata prevent full byte identity but do not alter frozen scientific outputs.

## Submission challenge

Page limit, abstract length, index terms, numbered citations, native equations,
figure/table callout order, caption placement, and PNG/TIFF availability pass automated
checks. Author, affiliation, funding, conflict, contribution, prior-publication, DOI,
and final disclosure fields remain human responsibilities.
